# Marketing Operations & Growth Systems

A practical collection of reusable playbooks, prompts, worksheets, and review tools for digital marketing and e-commerce work. The projects connect planning, creative execution, customer journeys, measurement, and operational follow-through.

## Toolkits

1. [Arabic Market Copy Localizer](arabic-market-copy-localizer/README.md) — adapt campaign copy to a defined Arabic-speaking market, audience, channel, and brand voice while keeping product and offer facts intact.
2. [E-commerce Unit Economics](ecommerce-unit-economics/README.md) — model order contribution and break-even acquisition cost from a store's own costs, payment mix, delivery outcomes, and advertising inputs.
3. [WhatsApp Commerce Conversations](whatsapp-order-follow-up/README.md) — organize order updates, customer follow-up, payment-option explanations, and support handoffs around consent and store policy.
4. [Commerce Event Tracking QA](commerce-measurement-planning/README.md) — map browser and server events, review consent and deduplication, and reconcile platform diagnostics with order records.
5. [Snapchat Campaign Planning](snapchat-campaign-planning/README.md) — plan account-specific creative tests, measurement, and launch checks using current platform guidance.
6. [MENA Advertising Pre-Flight Review](mena-ad-compliance-precheck/README.md) — structure evidence checks and review questions by product, market, audience, and advertising activity.

## Working with the materials

Start with the relevant toolkit README, replace worksheet fields with current business inputs, and record the source and date for facts that can change. Validate platform details against current official documentation and route legal or regulated questions to an appropriately qualified reviewer.

Examples are illustrative unless explicitly identified otherwise. These materials are reusable working resources; they do not claim a client deployment or measured outcome.
