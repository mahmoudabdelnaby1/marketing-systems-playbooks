# Marketing Operations & Growth Systems

A practical collection of reusable playbooks, prompts, worksheets, and review tools for digital marketing, e-commerce, customer experience, and measurement. These are working aids; they do not claim client deployment or measured results unless evidence is explicitly supplied.

## Toolkits

01. [Arabic Market Copy Localizer](arabic-market-copy-localizer/README.md) — Localize campaign copy using verified product facts and market review.
02. [E-commerce Unit Economics](ecommerce-unit-economics/README.md) — Model contribution and break-even acquisition from store inputs.
03. [WhatsApp Commerce Conversations](whatsapp-order-follow-up/README.md) — Plan customer messaging, consent, and support handoffs.
04. [Commerce Event Tracking QA](commerce-measurement-planning/README.md) — Map and reconcile commerce events across browser, server, and orders.
05. [Snapchat Campaign Planning](snapchat-campaign-planning/README.md) — Plan account-specific creative tests and launch checks.
06. [MENA Advertising Pre-Flight Review](mena-ad-compliance-precheck/README.md) — Organize claim evidence and human review questions.
07. [Arabic Search & AI Citation Review](arabic-aeo-geo-visibility-kit/README.md) — Record the same representative questions, language, date, answer, and visible citations before comparing changes.
08. [Arabic Merchant Center Feed Review](arabic-gmc-feed-architect/README.md) — Keep a source copy, reconcile each item against store records, then address current Merchant Center diagnostics by root cause.
09. [Arabic RTL Experience Review](arabic-rtl-cro-auditor/README.md) — Test real Arabic and mixed-language tasks, validation, loading, and recovery; inspect both mobile and desktop.
10. [GCC B2B Account-Based Campaign Planner](gcc-b2b-linkedin-abm-engine/README.md) — Map accounts to buying roles, align messages to needs, and test lead routing with sales and CRM.
11. [Agency Scope & Proposal Builder](mena-agency-pricing-proposal-engine/README.md) — Estimate resources per deliverable, calculate from chosen assumptions, and review scope, exclusions, and obligations.
12. [Cross-Market Paid Media Planning](mena-cross-border-ppc-guardrails/README.md) — Separate planning assumptions from account results; start with a measurable test and record assumptions and dates.
13. [Real Estate Lead Journey Planner](mena-real-estate-lead-architect/README.md) — Collect only necessary fields; distinguish inquiry, qualified lead, and completed viewing in CRM records.
14. [Seasonal Commerce Campaign Planner](mena-white-friday-11-11-war-room/README.md) — Calculate discount impact from inputs, review stock, pages, and tracking, then assign monitoring and stop rules.
15. [Salla & Zid Lifecycle Messaging Planner](salla-zid-klaviyo-retention-architect/README.md) — Verify account events and payloads, configure exclusions and frequency, and run internal tests before launch.
16. [TikTok Spark & Creator Brief Planner](tiktok-spark-ugc-mena-factory/README.md) — Approve the claims and brief, document paid usage rights, territory and dates, then verify authorization in TikTok before launch.

## How to use

Start with the relevant README, replace example fields with current business inputs, and record sources and dates for changing facts. Verify platform behavior in official documentation and route legal or regulated decisions to qualified reviewers. Illustrative examples are not benchmarks or promises.
