## Pre-publication checks

- [ ] Confirm the product and advertising activity are classified correctly.
- [ ] Check current creator licensing/permit requirements for this market and activity.
- [ ] Confirm ad disclosure, content approval, and platform-policy requirements.
- [ ] Attach evidence for factual, efficacy, comparative, price, and availability claims.
- [ ] Confirm any required regulatory or facility approval from the responsible authority.
- [ ] Define deliverable, usage rights, edits, cancellation, payment, and reporting terms in writing.
- [ ] Confirm consent, data minimization, and retention if campaign reporting includes personal data.
