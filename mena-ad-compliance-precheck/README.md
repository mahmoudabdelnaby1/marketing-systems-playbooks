# MENA Advertising Pre-Flight Review

A practical checklist for reviewing advertising copy, creator briefs, and campaign assets before publication. It helps teams identify claims that need evidence, product or audience details that are missing, and questions to confirm with the relevant authority or qualified local counsel.

## What it does

- Collects product classification, market, channel, audience, and approval status.
- Flags absolute, medical, comparative, and performance claims for evidence review.
- Prompts a human reviewer to confirm current market-specific requirements.
- Keeps the review record and unresolved questions together.

This is a planning aid, not legal advice, a regulator approval, or an automated compliance decision. Requirements depend on product category, channel, jurisdiction, and current rules. Do not publish solely on the basis of this checklist.

## Suggested workflow

1. Identify the exact product/service, market, channel, and intended audience.
2. Attach substantiation for each factual or comparative claim.
3. Check current primary-source requirements for that product and market.
4. Record questions that need regulator or legal review; do not infer approval.
5. Save the approved final asset and reviewer/date as a separate record.

The checklist helps organize a human review; it does not determine whether a specific asset complies with current law.
