# Egypt: Advertising Review Checklist

This file is a source-first checklist, not a statement of legal conclusions.

Before publishing content involving medicines, medical devices, cosmetics, supplements, or healthcare services:

1. Classify the exact product/service and identify the responsible regulator.
2. Locate current primary-source requirements for the category and the exact advertising medium.
3. Confirm whether product registration, ad-specific approval, professional licensing, or other permissions apply. Do not infer one approval from another.
4. Substantiate efficacy, safety, comparative, pricing, and availability claims.
5. Record the reviewer, source, date checked, and any unresolved legal questions.

Check current EDA publications and platform ad policies directly before relying on a requirement. This document does not summarize a specific enforcement list and does not claim that any listed restriction applies to every product or advertisement.
