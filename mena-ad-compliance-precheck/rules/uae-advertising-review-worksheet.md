# United Arab Emirates: Advertising Review Worksheet

Requirements can vary by emirate, product, service, creator, and advertising activity. This worksheet intentionally avoids a one-size-fits-all legal matrix.

## Review steps

1. Identify the emirate, product/service classification, advertiser, creator, channel, and intended audience.
2. Check current official guidance from the authority responsible for that exact activity. Starting points: [UAE Media Council Advertiser Guide](https://uaemc.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Advertiser-Guide.pdf), [permit information](https://uaemc.gov.ae/en/media-services/a-permit-for-a-natural-person-to-provide-advertising-or-media-content-in-social-media-and-other-modern-technical-means-individuals/), and [current FAQs](https://uaemc.gov.ae/faq/).
3. For health-related content, identify the relevant health regulator and check whether any approval applies to the product, advertiser, facility, creator, and/or specific content.
4. Record source, date checked, written approvals, required disclosures, and unresolved questions.

Do not generalize a permit requirement to every creator or post, or a healthcare-content rule to other categories. Obtain qualified local advice when scope is unclear.
