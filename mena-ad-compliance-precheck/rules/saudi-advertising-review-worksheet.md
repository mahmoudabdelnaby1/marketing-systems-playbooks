# Saudi Arabia: Advertising Review Worksheet

This file does not reproduce a comprehensive Saudi legal matrix. Identify the relevant product category and activity, then verify current requirements from the responsible Saudi authority before publication.

## Scope checks

- **Medical devices:** consult the [SFDA medical-device advertising requirements](https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en/regulations/requirements-advertisement-approval-and-launching-awareness-and-charity-campaigns). This source is specific to medical devices; it does not establish rules for every medicine, clinic, cosmetic, supplement, or consumer product.
- **Other regulated products/services:** classify the product and identify the responsible authority using its current official guidance. Do not infer that approval for sale is approval for a specific advertisement.
- **Creators and endorsements:** check current licensing, disclosure, and platform requirements for the creator's location and the specific promotional activity.
- **Personal data:** document purpose, lawful basis/consent where applicable, data minimization, access, retention, and deletion. Check current PDPL guidance with qualified counsel.
