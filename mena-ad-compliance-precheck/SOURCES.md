# Source Register

Use primary sources first. Confirm that the source is current and applies to the exact product, activity, channel, and jurisdiction. A link in this register is a starting point, not proof that a claim in a draft is compliant.

## Official sources

- Saudi Food and Drug Authority, medical-device advertising requirements: https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en/regulations/requirements-advertisement-approval-and-launching-awareness-and-charity-campaigns — scope is medical devices; do not generalize it to all health or consumer advertising.
- Saudi Food and Drug Authority, products requiring approval: https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en/news/17433 — use the current regulator guidance to establish product scope.
- UAE Media Council, Advertiser Guide: https://uaemc.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Advertiser-Guide.pdf
- UAE Media Council, advertiser permit information: https://uaemc.gov.ae/en/media-services/a-permit-for-a-natural-person-to-provide-advertising-or-media-content-in-social-media-and-other-modern-technical-means-individuals/
- UAE Media Council FAQs: https://uaemc.gov.ae/faq/

## Research discipline

For Egypt and other market-specific questions, locate the current regulator source for the product category and advertising activity before drafting a rule. Secondary commentary can help locate primary documents but should not be treated as the controlling source.
