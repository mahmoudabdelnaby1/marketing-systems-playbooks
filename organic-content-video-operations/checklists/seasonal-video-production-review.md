# Seasonal video production review

Use this checklist when planning seasonal video or a multi-format story. It complements the editorial/video brief; it does not prescribe a universal Ramadan schedule, viewing window, or media result.

## Story and audience

- [ ] Define the audience, question or need, content job, channel, format, and intended next action.
- [ ] Tie the story to an approved product/service fact or a real audience insight. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses.
- [ ] Decide whether the story should be episodic, standalone, demonstrative, explanatory, or service-led; do not force a series when the idea does not support one.
- [ ] Review cultural references and language with a fluent local reviewer for each intended market.

## Production and derivatives

- [ ] Build a shot list, script, run time, required assets, production owner, review dates, and delivery specifications from the current platform guidance.
- [ ] Capture a strong opening and a complete story that still makes sense without sound; add reviewed captions and accessible contrast.
- [ ] Plan channel-specific edits from the source footage. Reframe and re-edit rather than simply cropping a master into every placement.
- [ ] Confirm rights for people, locations, music, logos, customer material, AI-generated elements, and each organic or paid use.
- [ ] Confirm claims, disclosures, CTA, destination, and comment/support ownership on the final rendered files.

## Publish and learn

- [ ] Preview every language, ratio, duration, caption, thumbnail, and destination on a representative mobile device.
- [ ] Record asset/version, platform/account, publish time, owner, approval, metric definitions, and the baseline used for later comparison.
- [ ] Use actual audience and account data to choose timing. Do not assume a universal fasting, evening, or holiday viewing peak.
- [ ] After release, compare equivalent formats and audiences; separate reach, viewing, engagement, and business outcomes.
