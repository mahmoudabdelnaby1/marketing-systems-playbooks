# Organic Content & Video Operations Planner

A workflow for planning useful organic content and turning approved source material into channel-appropriate formats. This is distinct from the existing paid TikTok creator and Spark Ads toolkit.

## Use it when

A team needs to connect audience needs, editorial direction, production capacity, platform format, approvals, and learning across social posts and video.

## Included

- [`templates/editorial-and-video-brief.md`](templates/editorial-and-video-brief.md): reusable brief and approval record.
- [`templates/repurposing-matrix.csv`](templates/repurposing-matrix.csv): plan derivatives, rights, format, and measurement.

## Workflow

1. Choose an audience question using customer or sales evidence and state who the content serves.
2. Select a content job such as explain, demonstrate, answer, show a process, share substantiated proof, or invite an action. Do not force a fixed content ratio.
3. Brief the message, facts and sources, format, opening, sequence, CTA, accessibility needs, and claims to avoid.
4. Plan production, edits, crops, captions, language/register, reviewers, and delivery formats. Adapt each derivative to its destination.
5. Clear permissions for people, locations, music, logos, customer material, and organic/paid usage before publishing.
6. Record the live URL, date, owner, response path, metric definitions, and review date.
7. Compare like with like, state limitations, and choose one next iteration. Do not claim causality from correlation.

## Boundaries

This is a planning aid, not a promise of reach, virality, engagement, or lower acquisition cost. Do not invent customer stories, testimonials, or performance results. Check current platform guidance and the actual account before publishing or promoting content.

For time-bound campaigns, use the [Seasonal Video Production Review](checklists/seasonal-video-production-review.md) to coordinate the story, local review, production, rights, versions, and learning without assuming a universal holiday schedule or viewing peak.
