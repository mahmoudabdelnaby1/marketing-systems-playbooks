## Cost-control process

1. Audit actual delivered-message counts and invoices, not draft estimates.
2. Separate Meta charges from BSP, platform, and integration fees.
3. Send only messages that match the customer's opt-in and the purpose of the approved template.
4. Remove duplicate, stale, or unnecessary notifications while preserving support and order-service needs.
5. Test wording and timing against a documented baseline; assess complaints, opt-outs, delivery, and business outcomes together.

Do not reclassify promotional content as utility/service content to obtain a lower rate. Do not promise that a particular reply button, window, or category will make messages free; verify the applicable rules in current official documentation.
