# Customer message examples

**Templates only:** adapt to the customer’s actual order state, permissions, language, and approved merchant policy. Do not send if the customer has opted out or the required permission is absent. Include an approved opt-out instruction only when the sending setup can reliably honor it.

## Cart reminder

“Hi {{first_name}}, you left {{item_summary}} in your cart. If you still want it, you can review your cart here: {{cart_link}}. If you prefer not to receive further updates, let us know here.”

## Cash-on-delivery order confirmation

“Hello {{first_name}}, we received order {{order_reference}} for {{order_summary}}. The current total is {{verified_total}} {{currency}}. Please confirm whether you would like us to prepare it. You can review or update delivery details here: {{secure_link}}.”

## Delivery update

“Your order {{order_reference}} is scheduled for {{verified_delivery_window}}. If this timing does not work, reply here and our team can help.”

## Handoff

“I can connect you with a team member to review this. What is the best time to reach you?”

## Before sending

Verify the item, order status, total, delivery window, and link. Do not imply guaranteed delivery or invent discounts, stock urgency, fees, return terms, or financing eligibility.
