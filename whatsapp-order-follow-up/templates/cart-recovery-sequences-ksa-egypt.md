# Cart Follow-Up Message Templates

These drafts require adaptation to the store's actual opt-in, approved WhatsApp template category, message rules, product data, and customer-support policy. Verify current Meta and provider pricing before launch. Timing should follow consent, customer expectations, and measured results—not a universal schedule.

## Saudi Arabic draft

مرحبًا {{customer_name}}، تركت بعض المنتجات في سلتك لدى {{store_name}}. يمكنك مراجعة التفاصيل وإكمال الطلب من هنا: {{checkout_link}}.

إذا كان لديك سؤال، رد على هذه الرسالة وسنساعدك. لإيقاف رسائل العروض، اكتب {{opt_out_keyword}}.

## Egyptian Arabic draft

أهلًا {{customer_name}}، لسه في منتجات في سلتك عند {{store_name}}. تقدر تراجع التفاصيل وتكمل الطلب من هنا: {{checkout_link}}.

لو عندك سؤال، رد علينا وهنساعدك. لإيقاف رسائل العروض، اكتب {{opt_out_keyword}}.

## English draft

Hi {{customer_name}}, you left items in your cart at {{store_name}}. Review the details and continue checkout here: {{checkout_link}}.

Questions? Reply and our team can help. To stop promotional messages, reply {{opt_out_keyword}}.

## Before sending

- Confirm the recipient's opt-in and current template/category approval.
- Use accurate product names, prices, delivery terms, and availability from the store.
- Do not add false scarcity or unapproved discounts.
- Record delivered messages, opt-outs, complaints, and reconciled delivered orders.
- Review message cost against the current Meta rate card and provider invoice.
