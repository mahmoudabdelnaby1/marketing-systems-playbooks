# Payment-Method Information Message Drafts

Use these drafts only when the customer has asked about payment options or the business has an approved, policy-compliant reason to send the message. Show accurate fees, eligibility, and provider terms from the merchant's current contracts. Do not pressure customers or imply a payment method guarantees faster delivery or better outcomes unless the store can substantiate it.

## Arabic

يمكنك اختيار طريقة الدفع المتاحة لك عند إتمام الطلب: {{payment_options}}. تختلف الشروط والرسوم حسب الطريقة، ويمكنك مراجعتها هنا: {{terms_link}}. إذا أردت مساعدة، يسعدنا الرد على أسئلتك.

## English

You can choose from the payment methods available at checkout: {{payment_options}}. Terms and fees may vary by method; review them here: {{terms_link}}. Reply if you would like help.

## Review before use

- Verify provider fees and any applicable restrictions with current official/provider sources.
- Make payment options genuinely optional and display the total price clearly.
- Do not add a fee to one method or waive it for another without confirming legal, contractual, and platform requirements.
- Confirm opt-in and the correct WhatsApp template category before sending.
