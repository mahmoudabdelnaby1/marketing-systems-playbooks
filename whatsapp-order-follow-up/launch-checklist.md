# Launch checklist

- [ ] Confirm the WhatsApp Business product, provider, and approved sending number.
- [ ] Review current opt-in, template, message-category, quality, and pricing rules from official Meta/provider documentation.
- [ ] Verify message text, placeholders, links, language, and opt-out behavior with test records.
- [ ] Confirm a human handoff path and escalation owner.
- [ ] Confirm order and delivery data comes from the source system.
- [ ] Review privacy notice, permissions, retention, and access to customer conversations.
- [ ] Define separate metrics for delivery, reply, confirmation, cancellation, delivered order, refund, and complaint.
- [ ] Start with a controlled rollout and pause if data, consent, or customer experience checks fail.
