# Illustrative Conversation Flow Structure

> This is a fictional structure example. It is not a deployed integration, an approved template, measured customer behavior, or a pricing example.

## 1. Order confirmation

Use the order number, order total, delivery estimate supplied by the store, and clear confirm/cancel choices. Offer a support route for corrections or questions.

## 2. Cart follow-up

Send only where the customer has the required opt-in and the message meets current policy. Identify the cart without inventing product details. Provide a direct path to resume checkout and an easy way to stop marketing messages.

## 3. Human handoff

Escalate product, delivery, payment, refund, or complaint questions when the assistant cannot answer from the store's approved facts. Do not fabricate urgency, discount availability, stock, guarantees, or delivery times.

## Measurement plan

Track eligible contacts, delivered messages, opt-outs, complaints, confirmed orders, delivered orders, and contribution after costs. Define the date range and baseline before comparing. Do not infer incremental impact from attributed sales alone.
