# Customer-support operating guide

Use these rules when handling order and product questions through WhatsApp.

- Check the order system before stating order status, payment, delivery, stock, or refund information.
- If a fact is unavailable or conflicting, say so and offer a human handoff.
- Never invent prices, fees, discounts, delivery promises, return terms, warranty, or eligibility.
- Collect only information needed to resolve the current request; do not ask for payment-card details, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information.
- Treat address and phone information as personal data. Use approved systems, access controls, and retention rules.
- Respect opt-outs and the customer’s preferred language.
- Do not classify or disguise a message to bypass platform rules. Use the current approved template/category flow.
- Log operational outcomes accurately; do not claim that a conversation caused a recovered sale unless the attribution method supports that statement.
