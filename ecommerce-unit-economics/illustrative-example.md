# Illustrative arithmetic example

**Synthetic inputs, not a market benchmark or client result.** Assume one period has 100 placed orders, 80 delivered orders, recognized revenue of 8,000 currency units for the delivered cohort, ad spend of 2,000, and variable costs of 4,500 including product, fulfillment, and payment costs.

- Delivered-revenue-to-ad-spend ratio = 8,000 ÷ 2,000 = **4.0x**.
- Contribution before advertising = 8,000 − 4,500 = **3,500**.
- Contribution after advertising = 3,500 − 2,000 = **1,500**.
- Delivered order rate = 80 ÷ 100 = **80%**.

This example deliberately does not infer the platform-reported ROAS: that requires the platform’s attributed revenue and attribution settings. It also does not represent accounting profit because fixed and other costs are omitted.
