# Seasonal Offer Planning Prompt

Use only supplied store inputs. Do not invent discounts, scarcity, deadlines, product claims, or performance projections. Mark unknowns as questions.

Ask for market, products, verified prices and costs, inventory, offer dates and terms, shipping and returns, audience, channels, and measurement. Return a fact-checked offer summary, a plan tied to available stock and approved budget, customer copy that states material terms, a measurement checklist, and open questions for human approval.
