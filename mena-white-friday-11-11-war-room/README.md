# Seasonal Commerce Campaign Planner

Coordinate a seasonal launch from stock, margin, and offer readiness to product pages, measurement, and stop conditions.

## Use it when
Coordinate a seasonal launch from stock, margin, and offer readiness to product pages, measurement, and stop conditions.

## Prepare
Actual prices and costs, tax treatment, inventory, offer terms, order history, budget, team owners, and approval dates.

## Included
A store-readiness checklist, blank scenario template, and prompt for truthful seasonal messaging.

## Workflow
1. Calculate discount impact from inputs, review stock, pages, and tracking, then assign monitoring and stop rules.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
Scenarios are replaceable arithmetic examples, not forecasts. Verify offer terms and account-specific promotion eligibility.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
