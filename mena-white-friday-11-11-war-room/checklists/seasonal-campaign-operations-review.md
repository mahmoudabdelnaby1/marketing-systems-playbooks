# Seasonal campaign operations review

Use this alongside the Seasonal Commerce Campaign Planner to coordinate a seasonal launch, regardless of the occasion. Set dates from the actual campaign brief; this checklist includes no fixed holiday calendar or forecast.

## Decision and readiness

- [ ] Record campaign objective, target audience, markets, start/end dates, owner, approval path, and decision deadline.
- [ ] Verify offer terms, reference price, eligibility, exclusions, tax treatment, stock, fulfilment capacity, payment options, and support hours with named owners.
- [ ] Calculate contribution and break-even using current store inputs; record assumptions and source dates. Do not use seasonal benchmark multipliers as forecasts.
- [ ] Confirm product, category, landing, checkout, and policy pages show the same offer, price, availability, and dates.
- [ ] Confirm tracking events and order IDs across browser/server and store records; preserve the pre-campaign baseline and test a real end-to-end flow.

## People, assets, and contingency

- [ ] Assign one accountable owner for media, creative, store, inventory, fulfilment, support, analytics, and approvals.
- [ ] Set review checkpoints against production lead time and actual platform specifications.
- [ ] Confirm rights, disclosures, language review, claims evidence, and approved versions for every asset.
- [ ] Agree monitoring cadence, stock/operational constraints, change authority, escalation contacts, and stop/pause conditions before launch.
- [ ] Prepare a dated fallback for unavailable stock, payment/checkout failure, delayed fulfilment, inaccurate offer copy, or tracking failure.

## Close and improve

- [ ] Reconcile platform delivery with store orders, cancellations, returns, payment settlement, and support capacity before evaluating outcomes.
- [ ] Record what changed, when, who approved it, and the evidence used.
- [ ] Close the offer and remove or update time-sensitive copy and links.
- [ ] Hold a post-campaign review that separates observed results from explanations and assigns next actions with owners and dates.
