# Seasonal Campaign Readiness Checklist

- Confirm inventory, product eligibility, price history, and offer terms with the store owner.
- Recalculate contribution from current costs, tax treatment, fees, delivery outcomes, and returns.
- Confirm landing pages, mobile checkout, payment options, and service coverage.
- Validate measurement against order records and test events.
- Assign owners, approval deadlines, budget limits, monitoring intervals, and stop conditions.
- Verify any platform promotion feature in the current account and official help.

This checklist does not determine legal eligibility or guarantee results.
