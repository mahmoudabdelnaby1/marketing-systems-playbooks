# Localization review checklist

## Meaning and facts
- [ ] Product, price, discount, dates, eligibility, shipping, warranty, and CTA match approved source information.
- [ ] No new guarantee, superlative, scarcity claim, result, or endorsement was added.
- [ ] The localized copy preserves the intended meaning and does not hide important conditions.

## Audience and language
- [ ] Market and audience are defined more specifically than “GCC” when needed.
- [ ] Register fits the brand and placement; slang is natural rather than decorative.
- [ ] A native reviewer checked unfamiliar idioms, gender/address forms, and cultural references.
- [ ] The copy remains understandable to the intended audience across the full ad.

## Launch and learning
- [ ] Platform format and current policy were checked in official documentation.
- [ ] Variants are tagged and the comparison plan is documented.
- [ ] Results will be reported with date range, spend/delivery context, and the actual outcome metric.
