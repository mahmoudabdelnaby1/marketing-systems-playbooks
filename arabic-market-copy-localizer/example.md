# Synthetic localization example

**Status:** Illustrative language exercise only. No client, campaign, or performance result is represented.

### Brief

A fictional home-goods store wants a short social ad for a storage organizer. Verified product fact supplied for this exercise: it has three adjustable shelves. No price, delivery promise, warranty, or discount was provided.

### Source idea

“Make room for the things you use every day. The organizer has three adjustable shelves.”

### Neutral Arabic draft

“رتّب أغراضك اليومية بطريقة أوضح. المنظّم فيه ثلاثة أرفف قابلة للتعديل.”

### Gulf-leaning draft for native review

“خلّ أغراضك اليومية مرتّبة وقريبة منك. المنظّم فيه ثلاثة أرفف تقدر تعدّلها حسب احتياجك.”

### Review notes

- The feature claim stays unchanged.
- No price, delivery speed, warranty, or outcome guarantee was added.
- Confirm that “خلّ” and the overall register fit the selected city, audience, and brand voice before publishing.
