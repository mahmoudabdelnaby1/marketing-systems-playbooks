## Review prompts

- Does the copy sound natural to the intended reader, rather than merely substituting vocabulary?
- Are dialect, formality, gender, and honorific choices appropriate for this brand and context?
- Are product facts, offer terms, and regulated claims unchanged and supported?
- Are idioms clear and safe when read outside the intended market?
- Has a local fluent reviewer approved the final version?
