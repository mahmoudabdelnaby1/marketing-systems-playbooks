# Egyptian Arabic to Gulf Arabic: Review Notes

This is a small editorial checklist, not a definitive dialect dictionary. Word choice varies by speaker, age, context, and brand voice. Confirm wording with the intended audience or a fluent local reviewer.

| Draft element | Review question | Safer editorial action |
|---|---|---|
| Local time words such as `دلوقتي` | Is this the target market's natural phrasing for this brand? | Ask the reviewer; consider a neutral phrasing such as `الآن` when suitable. |
| Address terms such as `يا فندم` | Does this level of formality fit the audience and channel? | Match the brand's approved customer-service voice. |
| Delivery phrases such as `لحد باب البيت` | Is the wording natural and is the delivery promise verified? | Localize after confirming the actual service terms. |
| Product quality or origin language | Is each objective claim supported by the business? | Retain evidence-backed facts; remove unsupported superlatives. |
| Payment, financing, or return terms | Are the current market-specific terms confirmed? | Use the merchant's approved, current terms verbatim. |
| Health, beauty, or financial outcomes | Is the claim permitted and substantiated for this exact product and market? | Escalate to the compliance owner before publication. |

Avoid blanket claims that a word is used only in one country. Do not use this document as regulatory, payment-market, or consumer-behavior guidance.
