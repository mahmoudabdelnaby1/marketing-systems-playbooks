## Copy review

- Use only verified availability, pricing, discount terms, and delivery promises.
- Match register and dialect to the audience, channel, and brand voice; obtain local review.
- Check cultural and religious context with a knowledgeable reviewer.
- Treat seasonal timing and creative variations as hypotheses; evaluate using the account's own baseline and reconciled results.
