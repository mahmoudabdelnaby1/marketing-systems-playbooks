# Cross-Market Copy Review

Use this review after adapting copy between Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the Gulf, or another market. A dialect label is not a substitute for local audience knowledge or a qualified native review.

## Source and intent

- Keep the source copy, intended audience, channel, objective, and brand voice.
- Record which facts, claims, offer terms, prices, dates, and conditions must remain unchanged.
- Separate required meaning from wording that may be adapted.

- Check local vocabulary, tone, formality, idioms, gender/address forms, numerals, currency, and date formats.
- Do not label a version “Saudi,” “Gulf,” or “Egyptian” without specifying the intended audience and register.
- Flag a phrase as unresolved when evidence or reviewer confidence is insufficient. Do not guess.
- Keep product facts, eligibility, guarantees, health/financial claims, and legal terms unchanged unless the responsible owner approves the substantiated local adaptation.

## Final review

- Have a qualified in-market reviewer read the final copy in its actual placement and design.
- Compare CTA, landing page, offer conditions, and support responses for consistency.
- Record version, reviewer, approval date, and changes after approval.
- Verify Arabic shaping, RTL layout, mixed-language strings, punctuation, and mobile rendering.

This is a workflow aid, not legal approval or a guarantee of cultural fit or campaign performance.
