# Arabic campaign copy development brief

Use this brief to develop localized copy from an approved source. Keep the source facts visible during drafting and review.

## Inputs

- Source copy: [paste approved copy]
- Product/service and verified features: [details]
- Audience: [who and in what context]
- Target market and city/region if relevant: [market]
- Desired register: [Egyptian Arabic / Modern Standard Arabic / Gulf / other specified variety]
- Channel and placement: [channel]
- Objective: [awareness / consideration / lead / purchase / retention]
- Approved offer and terms: [details or “no offer”]
- Required CTA and destination: [details]
- Words, claims, or topics to avoid: [details]

## Drafting instructions

1. Keep product facts, offer terms, numbers, and eligibility unchanged.
2. Develop three distinct options appropriate to the requested register; do not stereotype the audience or force slang.
3. For each option, include the copy, intended tone, plain-language meaning, and any phrase that needs native review.
4. Flag unsupported claims, missing terms, and ambiguity instead of filling gaps.
5. Do not promise performance, legal compliance, medical outcomes, or platform approval.
6. If the requested variety is uncertain, explain the uncertainty and offer a neutral alternative for review.
