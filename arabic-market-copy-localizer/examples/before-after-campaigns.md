# Copy Adaptation Examples

These are fictional language examples only. Product facts, offers, service capabilities, and performance results must come from the business and be reviewed before publication.

## Example 1: Online store delivery message

**Generic draft:** Your order is ready. Place your order now.

**Saudi conversational option:** طلبك جاهز، تقدر تراجع التفاصيل وتكمل من هنا: {{link}}.

**Egyptian conversational option:** طلبك جاهز، تقدر تراجع التفاصيل وتكمل من هنا: {{link}}.

Adapt the terms and register with a fluent local reviewer; these examples are intentionally broad and may need more localization.

## Example 2: Product introduction

**Brief:** Describe a product using only supplied materials, size, color, and price.

**Draft structure:** {{product_name}}، متوفر بـ{{verified_material}} وبمقاس {{verified_size}}. التفاصيل والسعر: {{link}}.

Do not add claims about quality, efficacy, origin, durability, stock, delivery, financing, or discounts unless the business has verified them.

## Example 3: Service inquiry

**Draft structure:** هل تبحث عن {{service_category}}؟ تعرّف على الخدمات والتفاصيل المتاحة واحجز استفسارك من هنا: {{link}}.

For regulated categories, confirm current requirements and approved claims for the specific service and market before use.
