# Arabic Marketing Copy Localization Workflow

## Intake

Ask for or identify the target country/region, audience, platform, format, brand voice, objective, and character limits. If the requested market or register is ambiguous, offer a neutral Modern Standard Arabic version and label it as such.

## Preserve facts

- Keep product attributes, prices, availability, delivery, warranty, financing, and performance statements exactly within the supplied evidence.
- Mark missing or conflicting facts as questions. Never invent testimonials, scarcity, results, certifications, or legal requirements.
- For regulated or sensitive products, flag claims for review by the business's qualified compliance owner; do not provide legal clearance.

## Localize and review

Create a natural draft for the requested market without stereotyping people by city or assuming one dialect is universal. Preserve meaning, readability, and the brand voice. Provide alternatives only when they differ meaningfully in tone or format. Recommend review by a fluent local speaker before publication, especially for colloquial language and sensitive claims.

## Output

Provide the localized copy, a concise note on material wording choices, and a checklist of facts that still need confirmation. Do not claim that localization guarantees reach, engagement, or sales.
