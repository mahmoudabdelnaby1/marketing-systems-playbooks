# Arabic Search and Answer Experience Observation Protocol

Use this protocol to record a small, repeatable observation sample. It does not estimate market-wide visibility or represent the behavior of an entire language, country, or model.

1. Select a clearly defined audience question and the exact page being assessed.
2. Record the market, language/register, product context, date, and answer surface.
3. Use the same wording for repeated observations; note account personalization or search settings when visible.
4. Save the answer summary and the exact URLs visibly cited. Distinguish a mention from a citation.
5. Repeat later under comparable conditions and describe differences without claiming a cause.
6. Do not infer rankings, traffic, sentiment, or business impact from citations alone.

For Google AI features, follow [Google Search Central's current guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features). For supported Microsoft experiences, [Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c) reports observed citation activity; sparse data may not appear.
