# Product Buying Guides and Entity Fact Sheets

Use this workflow to make product and service information easier to verify and compare for people and search systems. It supports content quality and observation; it does not guarantee rankings, AI answers, or citations.

## Build from evidence

1. Define the reader’s decision and the exact products, services, market, and language in scope.
2. Collect current facts from accountable sources: product catalog, approved service records, warranty/returns policy, availability, and dated prices where relevant.
3. Separate verified facts, editorial explanation, user-provided information, and unknowns. Do not fill gaps with generated assumptions.
4. Organize the guide around decision questions: suitability, options, differences, constraints, total cost, next step, and source date.
5. Link each comparison field to a source of truth. Update or remove details when the source changes.
6. Keep product feeds, visible page details, structured data, and destination URLs consistent. Validate against current official platform requirements.
7. Review usefulness with the intended audience and accessibility needs. Record corrections and ownership.
8. If studying search or AI answers, use the same dated questions and record exact answers and citations as observations, not market-wide proof.

## Review boundaries

Do not make unsupported health, safety, financial, legal, comparative, or performance claims. Regulated content requires qualified review. A useful guide may support discoverability, but no format or schema guarantees placement or citation.
