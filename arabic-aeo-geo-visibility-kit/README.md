# Arabic Search & AI Citation Review

Review Arabic or English pages for discoverability, clarity, and citations in search and AI-assisted answer experiences.

## Use it when
Review Arabic or English pages for discoverability, clarity, and citations in search and AI-assisted answer experiences.

## Prepare
Page URL, language, audience, query, indexation state, page content, and available measurement sources.

## Included
A bilingual observation log and repeatable sample protocol for recording answers, links, and dates.

## Workflow
1. Record the same representative questions, language, date, answer, and visible citations before comparing changes.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
A sample is observational, not a market estimate, and cannot guarantee rankings or citations. Google says existing SEO fundamentals still apply and requires no special optimization for its AI features.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
