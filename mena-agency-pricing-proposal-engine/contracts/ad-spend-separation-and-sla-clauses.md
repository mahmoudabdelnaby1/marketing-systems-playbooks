# Commercial Terms Discussion Checklist

Use these topics to prepare a contract for review; they are not ready-to-sign legal clauses.

- Separate professional fees from media spend, creator fees, taxes, and third-party costs. State who pays each provider and when.
- Define deliverables, channels, quantities, acceptance steps, approval deadlines, and the process for scope changes.
- Define access, data handling, confidentiality, retention, and return of account materials.
- Set service hours, response expectations, escalation contacts, dependencies, and agreed reporting cadence.
- Specify term, renewal, termination, outstanding charges, and the treatment of prepaid spend.
- Have jurisdiction-specific terms reviewed by qualified counsel before signature.

All amounts, timelines, remedies, and service levels must be negotiated for the engagement; no default rates or obligations are implied.
