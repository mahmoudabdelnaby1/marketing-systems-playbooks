# Agency Scope & Proposal Builder

Build a client proposal from actual delivery scope, time, costs, and responsibilities.

## Use it when
Build a client proposal from actual delivery scope, time, costs, and responsibilities.

## Prepare
Team hours and loaded cost, direct expenses, responsibilities, workload, term, and agreed service boundaries.

## Included
A bilingual proposal template, blank cost inputs, and negotiable clauses for media spend and service levels.

## Workflow
1. Estimate resources per deliverable, calculate from chosen assumptions, and review scope, exclusions, and obligations.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
No market rate or recommended margin is asserted. Examples are not client engagements; have contract and tax terms reviewed by qualified professionals.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
