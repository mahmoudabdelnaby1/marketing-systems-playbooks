# Design review scenarios

Use these to review a proposed design. They are scenarios, not claims about client performance.

1. **Pause and compare sales:** A campaign is paused for a fortnight and orders fall. The team calls the change incremental lift. **Review:** classify as before/after observational evidence unless a valid assignment and comparison plan existed; list other changes and outcome lags.
2. **National delivery with regional holdout:** Ads target the whole country while selected cities are treated as control. **Review:** flag likely exposure spillover; redesign delivery or choose a different method.
3. **COD orders counted on click date:** Orders are credited before collection and returns are excluded. **Review:** define the business outcome and lag, reconcile both groups against the same system of record.
4. **Threshold copied from an old deck:** A proposed platform test assumes a fixed conversion minimum from a prior account. **Review:** verify current availability and eligibility for this account in official documentation; keep the source date in the preregistration.
5. **Interim numbers look favorable:** The test is stopped as soon as the desired result appears. **Review:** apply registered stop rules; label an unplanned stop as a deviation and do not present it as the planned final read.
6. **One channel result generalized to all marketing:** A test supports additional outcomes for one campaign and time window. **Review:** scope the claim to that tested population and intervention; do not generalize to the whole budget.
7. **No meaningful outcome or decision:** The team can report a platform metric but cannot say which decision will change. **Review:** redesign the question or do not run the test.
