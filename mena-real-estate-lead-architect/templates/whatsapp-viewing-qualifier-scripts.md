# Property Inquiry and Viewing Message Templates

Use only verified availability, price, fees, payment terms, and viewing details. Ask only for information needed to respond to the customer's request.

## Arabic
أهلًا {{الاسم}}، شكرًا لاهتمامك بـ {{المشروع/الوحدة}}. هل تفضّل معرفة {{السعر/المساحة/الموقع/المتاح حاليًا}}؟ التفاصيل المتوفرة لدينا: {{حقائق مؤكدة مع تاريخها}}. إذا رغبت، يمكننا تنسيق معاينة في {{مواعيد مؤكدة}} أو ترتيب مكالمة.

## English
Hello {{name}}, thanks for asking about {{project/unit}}. Would you like details on {{price/size/location/current availability}}? Here is the information currently confirmed: {{verified facts and date}}. If useful, we can arrange a viewing at {{confirmed times}} or schedule a call.

Before sending, verify the unit is available and details match approved records. Do not promise returns, residency eligibility, financing approval, construction status, or delivery dates unless confirmed by an authorized source.
