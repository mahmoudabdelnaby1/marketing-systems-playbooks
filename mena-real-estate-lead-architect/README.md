# Real Estate Lead Journey Planner

Design a property inquiry journey connecting customer requirements to a viewing or sales handoff.

## Use it when
Design a property inquiry journey connecting customer requirements to a viewing or sales handoff.

## Prepare
Verified available units, locations and prices, qualification rules, approved financing options, viewing hours, and CRM.

## Included
Search-intent prompts, qualification questions, WhatsApp scripts, and a blank cost-per-viewing input sheet.

## Workflow
1. Collect only necessary fields; distinguish inquiry, qualified lead, and completed viewing in CRM records.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
Remove unsupported investment or legal promises. Do not populate calculations with assumed market costs or fabricated case outcomes.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
