# Modular Creator Concept Framework

Create alternative openings around a verified customer question or product demonstration. These are creative directions, not required formats or performance benchmarks.

| Angle | Creator direction | Evidence or approval needed |
|---|---|---|
| Customer problem | Describe a real, specific use situation | Confirm the issue and product relevance |
| Demonstration | Show the product or service in use | Confirm the demonstration is accurate |
| Comparison | Explain a meaningful difference | Substantiate each comparison and review local/platform rules |
| Offer | Explain an active offer and its terms | Confirm dates, availability, exclusions, and price |

Keep the creator's voice natural. Check current platform specifications, disclosure controls, permissions, and destination before launch. Do not present invented testimonials or imply guaranteed results.
