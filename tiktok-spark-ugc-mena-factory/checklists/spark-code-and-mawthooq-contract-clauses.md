# Spark Ads and Creator Rights Checklist

Document the creator, post URL, authorization method and expiry, paid-use duration, territory, edits, disclosure responsibilities, and fallback if the post or authorization becomes unavailable. Confirm current Spark setup in TikTok Ads Manager and local requirements with qualified counsel where relevant.

## Agreement questions
- Which channels and placements may use the content?
- For what dates and territories?
- Are edits, captions, translations, and cutdowns permitted?
- Who handles disclosure and approval?
- What happens when authorization expires or the post is removed?
- What files and usage rights are included?

Terms must be negotiated for the actual collaboration. This checklist is not legal advice and sets no default license duration.
