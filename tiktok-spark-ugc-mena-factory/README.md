# TikTok Spark & Creator Brief Planner

Plan a creator brief and authorized-post use in a paid TikTok campaign.

## Use it when
Plan a creator brief and authorized-post use in a paid TikTok campaign.

## Prepare
Goal, audience, product, substantiated claims, territory and duration of use, approvals, post URL, account settings.

## Included
A creator brief, creative-angle planning system, and authorization, rights, and disclosure checklist.

## Workflow
1. Approve the claims and brief, document paid usage rights, territory and dates, then verify authorization in TikTok before launch.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
There is no universal authorization duration or guaranteed result. Check the current Spark workflow, creator agreement, platform terms, and applicable local requirements.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
