# Customer Lifecycle Email Copy Framework (Arabic and English)

Customize using approved brand, product, privacy, and offer information. Send only under the store's consent and suppression rules.

## Welcome email
**Subject (Arabic):** أهلًا بك في {{اسم المتجر}}
**Subject (English):** Welcome to {{store name}}

**Arabic:** شكرًا لانضمامك. يمكنك استكشاف {{فئة/منتجات موثقة}} أو التواصل معنا عبر {{قناة الدعم}}. {{أضف العرض فقط إذا كان فعّالًا وتنطبق شروطه}}.

**English:** Thanks for joining us. Explore {{verified category/products}} or contact us through {{support channel}}. {{Include an offer only when active and disclose its terms}}.

## Post-purchase service message
Acknowledge the order using verified order status, link to the current support or order page, and explain how to get help.

## Re-engagement
Use only where permission and platform rules allow. State why the customer is receiving the message, offer a clear preference/unsubscribe path, and avoid implying a discount, review, deadline, or availability that is not confirmed.

Do not invent reviews, bestseller status, delivery promises, guarantees, discount codes, or results. Confirm event timing and suppression logic against the connected account.
