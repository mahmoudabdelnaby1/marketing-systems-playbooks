# Salla & Zid Lifecycle Messaging Planner

- Start with the decision, audience, market, and current account inputs. Ask for missing facts instead of filling them with assumptions.
- Keep examples illustrative and label them. Separate observations, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Check changing platform behavior against current official documentation.
- Do not claim legal approval, performance improvement, ranking, citation, or revenue outcomes.
- Preserve source data and record dates for prices, policies, and other changing facts.
- Refer regulated or contractual questions to a qualified reviewer.
