# Salla & Zid Lifecycle Messaging Planner

Plan email and WhatsApp messages around lifecycle stages and events actually available in the store.

## Use it when
Plan email and WhatsApp messages around lifecycle stages and events actually available in the store.

## Prepare
Integration source and version, sample payload, marketing consent, return policy, approved templates, escalation owner.

## Included
An event reference map, customizable message material, and a flow planning template linking event, message, and measurement.

## Workflow
1. Verify account events and payloads, configure exclusions and frequency, and run internal tests before launch.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
Do not assume every event or field exists in every store. Salla documents Klaviyo integration events; verify the connected account before building a flow.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
