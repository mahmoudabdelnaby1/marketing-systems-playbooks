# Arabic Merchant Center Feed Review

Review product data before Merchant Center submission and reconcile identifiers, prices, availability, and landing pages.

## Use it when
Review product data before Merchant Center submission and reconcile identifiers, prices, availability, and landing pages.

## Prepare
Current feed export, SKU and available GTIN or MPN, currency, price, stock, target country, and product URL.

## Included
A workflow for matching product data to the source, landing page, checkout, and current diagnostics.

## Workflow
1. Keep a source copy, reconcile each item against store records, then address current Merchant Center diagnostics by root cause.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
Never invent an identifier, price, or specification. Requirements vary by item, destination, and current data specification; this guide cannot guarantee approval.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
