# App Store Listing Research & Test Planner

Use this guide to turn app-store search and listing evidence into a focused, testable change plan for Google Play or the Apple App Store.

## Use it when

You manage an app listing and need to decide which audience, search intent, message, or visual asset deserves review or a controlled test.

## Prepare

Store, app and country; listing language; audience and user need; current listing assets and metadata; dated search, impression, visit and install data where available; product constraints and approved claims.

## Workflow

1. Define one user segment and its search or discovery intent.
2. Capture themes from representative customer research or available public review data; record the source, sample and selection limits. Treat themes as qualitative signals, not estimates of how common a view is.
3. Record the current listing, baseline period, available traffic and outcome metric; separate observed data from assumptions.
4. Write a hypothesis connecting one proposed change to a user need. Keep unrelated asset changes separate when the store experiment supports that.
5. Check current store rules, localization, asset requirements and experiment availability in official documentation.
6. Set a decision rule, minimum observation period and guardrails before launch; do not call a low-volume result a winner.
7. After the test, record the result, uncertainty, decision and next test. Preserve the original assets and source dates.

## Included

A listing evidence sheet, audience-and-intent brief, hypothesis and variant register, test decision log, and post-test review prompts. It does not scrape app stores, estimate guaranteed ranking, or assert a conversion lift.

## Review boundaries

Store capabilities and experiment rules change. Use current Apple and Google guidance, and interpret results using the platform’s metric definitions and uncertainty.
