# Arabic RTL Experience Review

Review Arabic and RTL interfaces on product and checkout pages before approving design or changes.

## Use it when
Review Arabic and RTL interfaces on product and checkout pages before approving design or changes.

## Prepare
Test URL or environment, device, browser, task steps, screenshots, and current storefront rules.

## Included
A task-based review checklist and CSS starter for bidirectional text and spacing checks.

## Workflow
1. Test real Arabic and mixed-language tasks, validation, loading, and recovery; inspect both mobile and desktop.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
The checklist does not predict conversion lift or replace user research. Confirm delivery, payment, and return claims with approved policy.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
