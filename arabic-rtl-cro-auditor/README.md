# Arabic RTL Experience Review

Review Arabic and mixed-language interfaces on product and checkout pages before approving design or changes.

## Use it when

You need to inspect a real Arabic or mixed-language product, cart, or checkout journey across mobile and desktop.

## Prepare

Test URL or environment, device, browser, language, task steps, enabled payment methods, approved storefront policies, screenshots, and current platform rules.

## Included materials

- `checklists/rtl-interface-review.md` — review language direction, mixed text, mobile behavior, validation, and recovery.
- `checklists/mobile-checkout-review.md` — record evidence across Arabic checkout, forms, price clarity, payments, recovery, and measurement.
- `snippets/rtl-bidi-and-mobile-checkout-fixes.css` — starter CSS; validate selectors on the actual storefront.

## Workflow

1. Test real Arabic and mixed-language tasks, validation, loading, and recovery; inspect both mobile and desktop.
2. Record observations with screenshot or reproducible-step evidence. Keep hypotheses and proposed fixes separate.
3. Prioritize issues by user impact, harm risk, and effort without inventing a score or promising conversion lift.
4. Review platform behavior, prices, delivery, payment, and return claims against current official guidance and approved store policy.
5. Validate changes with a method that fits available evidence; document seasonality, concurrent changes, and uncertainty before drawing a conclusion.

## Boundaries

This review does not predict conversion lift, certify legal compliance, or replace user research. Confirm delivery, payment, and return details with approved policy. Never recommend bypassing authentication, OTP, fraud, or payment controls.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
