# Short-Form Video Opening and Creative Test Worksheet

Develop openings that make the product, audience, and message clear. Use this worksheet to generate testable creative hypotheses; performance and fatigue vary by account, audience, placement, and campaign.

## Check current specifications

Before production, verify the current official Snapchat specifications for the selected ad format, aspect ratio, duration, safe zones, captions, audio, and destination. Do not rely on old character limits or unofficial platform guides.

## Build creative variants

For each concept, vary one opening idea while keeping the body, offer, audience, destination, and optimization event consistent where practical.

Do not use customer messages, faces, or testimonials without permission. Use only claims and offer terms the business can substantiate.

## Read results

Set the evaluation period, spend, and decision rule from the account's budget and expected volume before launch. Compare delivery, attention signals, qualified outcomes, and unit economics using the same reporting definitions. Do not pause or scale a variant from a small or non-comparable sample, and do not apply universal frequency or CTR thresholds.
