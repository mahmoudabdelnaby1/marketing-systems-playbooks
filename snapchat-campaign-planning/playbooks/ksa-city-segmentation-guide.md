# Geographic Creative Testing Worksheet

Use geography as a test variable only when delivery coverage, audience size, and the business offer support a fair comparison. City names are not audience insights by themselves.

## Before the test

- Confirm the locations where the business can actually sell and deliver.
- Check platform targeting availability and the account's current campaign setup.
- Define one business outcome, such as qualified leads, confirmed orders, or contribution margin.
- Keep offer, landing page, attribution window, and optimization event consistent across compared groups.
- Set spend and duration from the account's available budget and expected conversion volume; do not use a universal SAR threshold.

## Test plan

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | [locations] | [message or proof relevant to these customers] | [outcome] | [account-specific] | [record after sufficient data] |
| B | [locations] | [message or proof relevant to these customers] | [outcome] | [account-specific] | [record after sufficient data] |

Avoid changing location, creative, offer, and optimization settings at the same time. Note delivery limitations and uncertainty before interpreting a difference. Do not infer cultural preferences from city alone; validate copy with local customers or a fluent reviewer.

## Review

Compare spend, reach, frequency, clicks, landing-page behavior, qualified outcomes, cancellations/returns where relevant, and unit economics. Use the platform's current reporting definitions and the merchant's order records. Keep a variant only when the observed result is meaningful for the business and the test had enough comparable delivery.
