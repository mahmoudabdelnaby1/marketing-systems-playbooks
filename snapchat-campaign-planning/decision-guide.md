# Reading a campaign test

1. **Check data quality first:** confirm event firing, deduplication, attribution window, timezone, and spend alignment.
2. **Compare like with like:** keep objective, audience, placement, and offer consistent when isolating a creative change.
3. **Use business outcomes:** clicks and platform-reported events are diagnostic signals; compare qualified leads, delivered orders, or other agreed outcomes where available.
4. **Respect uncertainty:** small samples and short windows can produce unstable rates. Avoid treating an early fluctuation as a reliable winner.
5. **Record confounders:** stock, price, site changes, promotions, delivery coverage, and concurrent campaigns can affect results.
6. **State conclusions narrowly:** include the tested period, sample size, metric source, and limits. Do not generalize a single test to all GCC markets or categories.
