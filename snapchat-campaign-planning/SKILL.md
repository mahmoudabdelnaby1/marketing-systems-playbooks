# Snapchat Campaign Planning Workflow

## Purpose

Create a testable campaign plan using the advertiser's actual objectives, audience, offer, margin, and measurement setup. Do not assume lower costs, better outcomes, platform reach, or fixed budget/cadence rules. Label recommendations as hypotheses and define how they will be evaluated.

## Workflow

1. **Business and measurement brief:** clarify the outcome, market, audience, landing destination, order economics, tracking events, attribution window, and decision owner. Reconcile a baseline from account and order data.
2. **Audience and creative hypotheses:** propose a small set of audience and message variations based on supplied customer insight. Keep product claims and offer terms evidence-based. Confirm the creative format against current [Snap Business Help specifications](https://businesshelp.snapchat.com/s/article/top-snap-specs?language=en_US).
3. **Test design:** set a test budget the advertiser can afford, define the primary metric and guardrails, isolate meaningful variables, and choose a review point based on sufficient delivery and conversion data—not an arbitrary universal schedule.
4. **Launch QA and learning:** verify destination, disclosures, pixel/server events, consent, currency, and reporting. After the test, compare platform signals with reconciled backend outcomes and record what should be continued, changed, or stopped.

## Required output

- Objective and measurement definition
- Audience/creative hypotheses and rationale
- Account-specific budget and test duration
- QA checklist and unresolved dependencies
- Evaluation method, guardrails, and next decision

Do not present examples as live campaign results or guaranteed CPA/ROAS. Current platform policies and product availability can change; verify them before launch.
