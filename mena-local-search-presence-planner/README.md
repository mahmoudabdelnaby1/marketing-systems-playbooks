# MENA Multi-Location Local Search Planner

A branch-by-branch operating kit for keeping local business information accurate and making location pages useful to people who need a nearby service. It focuses on evidence and consistency; it does not promise map-pack positions or search rankings.

## Use it when

A business has multiple real locations and needs a repeatable way to review branch data, profiles, local pages, customer feedback, and measurement without publishing duplicate city pages.

## Included

- [`templates/branch-register.csv`](templates/branch-register.csv): blank source-of-truth register.
- [`checklists/location-page-review.md`](checklists/location-page-review.md): evidence-led page and technical review.

## Workflow

1. Build a verified register of active branches, approved details, source of truth, owner, and last-confirmed date.
2. Compare public profiles with approved records; route discrepancies to the accountable location owner.
3. Check that each page helps visitors with branch-specific services, access, contact, hours, and booking information. Avoid doorway pages that differ only by place name.
4. Review indexability, canonical destination, language routing, internal links, and structured data against visible, verified content.
5. Assign a feedback-response owner and privacy-aware escalation path.
6. Measure defined actions per branch and separate profile-reported interactions from completed business outcomes.
7. Keep a dated decision log with evidence, owner, approval, and follow-up date.

## Boundaries

This planner is not legal advice, a substitute for platform eligibility rules, or a ranking guarantee. Do not invent branches, addresses, reviews, service areas, or performance results. Check current official Google Business Profile and Search documentation before implementation.
