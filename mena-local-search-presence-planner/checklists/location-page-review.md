# Location Page Review

Use one copy per real branch. Record the URL, reviewer, review date, source documents, and unresolved questions before proposing edits.

## User information

- Does the page identify the actual branch and services available there?
- Are address, hours, phone, access instructions, and booking options current against an approved source?
- Can a visitor understand the next step on mobile?
- Does the page include useful local details rather than only replacing a city name?
- Are reviews, credentials, testimonials, and claims attributable and approved?

## Search and page integrity

- Is the page reachable, indexable when intended, and linked from a relevant location index?
- Does the canonical point to the intended page?
- Do language and regional versions connect correctly where both exist?
- Does structured data describe visible, verified content and the correct branch?
- Are titles, headings, internal links, images, and contact destinations accurate?
- Are duplicate or near-empty pages better consolidated or improved before publication?

## Measurement and approval

- Are calls, directions, bookings, or inquiries defined consistently?
- Are tagged links and events tested without treating a click as a completed customer outcome?
- Has the location owner confirmed operational details?
- Are evidence, proposed edits, and unresolved items recorded?
