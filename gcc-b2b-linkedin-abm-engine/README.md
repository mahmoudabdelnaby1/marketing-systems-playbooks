# GCC B2B Account-Based Campaign Planner

Plan B2B outreach around target accounts, buying roles, relevant messaging, offers, and a clear measurement path.

## Use it when
Plan B2B outreach around target accounts, buying roles, relevant messaging, offers, and a clear measurement path.

## Prepare
Approved account list, buying roles, offer, sales cycle, language, CRM, and actual budget.

## Included
An account and message planning template plus lead-form versus landing-page checks and CRM handoff steps.

## Workflow
1. Map accounts to buying roles, align messages to needs, and test lead routing with sales and CRM.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
Do not use generic budget or CPL benchmarks. LinkedIn form limits and fields can change; verify current specs and account settings.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.
