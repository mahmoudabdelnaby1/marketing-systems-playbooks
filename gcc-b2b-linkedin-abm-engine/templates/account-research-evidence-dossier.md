# B2B Account Research Evidence Dossier

Use the companion CSV to document why an account or buying-role hypothesis is relevant before outreach. It combines ICP research with a source-provenance and permitted-use review; it is not a lead scraper, a legal verdict, or proof that an inferred need is true.

## Evidence labels

- **Observed:** directly present in a dated source, such as a public company announcement or the supplied CRM record.
- **Inferred:** a reasoned hypothesis that still needs confirmation. State the evidence and confidence; never write it as a confirmed fact in a personalized message.
- **Unknown:** missing, stale, conflicting, or not checked. Do not fill gaps with generated assumptions.

## Review before adding a record to an outreach audience

1. Define the account-fit criteria and disqualifiers before researching accounts.
2. Record the exact source URL, source type, owner, observation date, and last verification date for each material fact.
3. Separate company-level fit evidence from a person's contact information. Minimize personal data and avoid collecting sensitive data.
4. Review the source's terms and the intended use for the market and channel. Mark `permitted_use_review_status` as `not reviewed`, `needs qualified review`, or `approved for stated use`; record who reviewed it and when.
5. Set a re-verification date. Remove or re-check stale or conflicting claims before use.
6. Keep opt-outs, suppression status, and contact authorization in the system of record used by the campaign; do not treat an account-fit signal as permission to contact.
7. Have a human approve the audience and message. Record the decision and CRM reference.

Do not use a composite fit score as a fact or let an AI-generated dossier create contact records automatically. Keep evidence and hypotheses traceable to their sources, and follow current platform, privacy, and local legal requirements.
