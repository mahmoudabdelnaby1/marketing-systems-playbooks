# LinkedIn Lead Form or Landing Page: Decision Checklist

Choose the destination that supports the agreed qualification and follow-up process. Do not assume one format always produces lower cost or better lead quality.

Compare the goal, qualification criteria, required fields and consent wording, mobile page experience, CRM mapping, owner and response process, current form specs, and measurement source. Test lead routing before launch and compare qualified outcomes with consistent definitions.

Review current specifications at [LinkedIn Lead Gen Ads](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/lead-gen-ads/specs). Account settings and available fields can change.
