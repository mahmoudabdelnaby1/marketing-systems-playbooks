# Paid-Account Restriction: Incident Record and Response Checklist

Use this checklist to organize facts after an ad account, business asset, payment method, or user access is restricted. It does not predict enforcement, prevent all restrictions, or guarantee reinstatement.

## 1. Identify the affected asset

- Platform and account/business asset ID:
- Asset owner and current authorized administrator:
- Exact status and notice text (preserve a copy; do not paraphrase as the platform's reason):
- Notice location, date, time, and timezone:
- Ads, destinations, billing events, verification requests, or access changes named in the notice:
- Current in-account actions offered and their deadline, if shown:

Do not infer that an ad rejection, payment hold, account restriction, and user-access restriction are the same issue. Confirm the affected asset in the platform's current account-status interface.

## 2. Secure and preserve

- Preserve the notice, relevant IDs, account-change history, billing records, policy version/link, and the exact affected ad/destination.
- Check administrator access and recent security alerts. If compromise is suspected, use the platform's account-security process before campaign changes.
- Pause or correct content only when the notice or an authorized reviewer indicates that action; record who made the change and when.
- Keep a factual timeline. Separate confirmed observations, team hypotheses, and unresolved questions.

## 3. Prepare a review or appeal, if offered

1. Read the platform's current notice and official policy page for the specific issue.
2. Correct verified problems first; do not change identity, ownership, or destinations to evade enforcement.
3. Assemble only requested, relevant documents. Remove unrelated personal or customer data.
4. Explain the account and asset IDs, the notice, the corrective action, and the evidence supporting the request. Be accurate; do not claim a platform error without evidence.
5. Submit through the platform's official in-account route when available. Record the submission ID, timestamp, status, and next owner.
6. Avoid duplicate submissions while a review is pending unless the platform instructs otherwise.

## 4. Close the incident

Record the decision, remaining restrictions, any operational changes, and lessons that can be verified from the incident. Re-check the live account before resuming spend. Do not create replacement accounts or assets to bypass a restriction.

## Official starting points

- [Google Ads account suspensions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640): causes, account state, and appeal guidance; follow the current notice and account-specific instructions.
- [Meta Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/): content standards. For account or asset restrictions, follow the current action shown in Meta Business Support Home.
- [Meta Business Support Home](https://www.facebook.com/business-support-home): current account-status and issue-resolution entry point.

Platform procedures change and may differ by issue, asset, account, and market. This operational record is not legal advice, a compliance certification, or a promise of account recovery.
