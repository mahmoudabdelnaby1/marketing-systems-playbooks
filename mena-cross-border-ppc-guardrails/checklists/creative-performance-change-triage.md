# Creative Performance Change Triage

Use this checklist when an ad's results change. It is a diagnostic sequence, not a threshold-based fatigue detector. Evaluate each platform and campaign against its own history, objective, delivery, and measurement setup.

## 1. Confirm the change

- Define the metric that changed, the comparison periods, timezone, attribution setting, and data source.
- Compare like-for-like campaign, audience, placement, objective, and creative versions where possible.
- Record spend and delivery volume. Small samples can make rates unstable.
- Separate platform-reported results from store, CRM, or qualified-lead outcomes.

## 2. Check measurement before changing creative

- Did event volume, consent behavior, browser/server deduplication, URLs, forms, or CRM routing change?
- Are clicks and landing sessions still arriving as expected?
- Did the conversion definition, attribution window, or reporting delay change?
- Preserve a known-good test path and assign an owner to investigate discrepancies.

## 3. Consider non-creative causes

Review auction competition, budget or bid changes, audience size/composition, placement mix, stock, pricing, offer, landing page, seasonality, delivery constraints, and sales follow-up. Use the account's own history and evidence; do not copy universal frequency, CTR, or decay cutoffs from another account.

Change one major factor at a time when practical. Preserve a control or reference version when the test design allows it. State what result would change the decision before launch.

## 5. Close the loop

Record the result, sample limitations, decision, owner, and next review date. Avoid declaring an ad “fatigued” from a single metric or attributing a recovery to a change when other factors moved at the same time.
