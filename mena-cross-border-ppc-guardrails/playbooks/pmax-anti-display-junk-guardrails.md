# Performance Max Review Checklist

Review campaign goals, asset groups, conversion actions, URL settings, brand controls, exclusions, and placement reporting currently available in the account. Confirm the conversion source against business records and check assets and destination pages before changing delivery.

Google documents current controls in [Performance Max brand suitability](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13607727?hl=en) and [asset group guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14104727?hl=en). Availability and reporting vary. This checklist does not promise exclusion of every placement or improved performance.
