# Cross-Market Paid Media Planning

Plan a paid media test for a new market using account, cost, currency, and local conversion inputs.

## Use it when
Plan a paid media test for a new market using account, cost, currency, and local conversion inputs.

## Prepare
Market and currency, account data, offer, landing page, conversion definition, contribution margin, and dated exchange rate.

## Included
A blank test-planning worksheet and asset, measurement, and campaign review checklist.

## Workflow
1. Separate planning assumptions from account results; start with a measurable test and record assumptions and dates.
2. Record sources, dates, unknowns, and the decision owner.
3. Review the output against the live account or business records before use.

## Boundaries
Do not use fixed CPC multipliers or market ratios. Verify current targeting and placement controls in the platform account.

Examples and worksheets are planning aids. They do not claim client deployment or measured outcomes unless supporting evidence is explicitly supplied.

For search-term exclusions, use the account-specific [`Arabic Negative Keyword Review`](guides/arabic-negative-keyword-review.md). It requires review of actual queries and current official matching guidance before applying exclusions.
